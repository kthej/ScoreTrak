# ScoreTrak - The most versatile scoring app the Garmin family has ever seen

## Marketing Description

ScoreTrak is the ULTIMATE scorekeeping app for the Garmin family.
You can record and track the scores of ANY type of game, from basketball to tennis to golf,
with the option to record your game as an activity.

What's more, is the app will save your scores to be viewable in Connect IQ!
Each time you make a score change, ScoreTrak will record that change in the activity file, enabling you to see how your game progressed as you

Need a simple scorekeeper without all the extra tools? ScoreTrak's got you with its super simple and easy to use layout!

Need a power tool for recording your group's weekly sport or game night? ScoreTrak's got you with its incredibly diverse array of options and scorekeeping modes!

## Overview

- Extremely simple scorekeeping app
- Keeps track of Home and Guest in a clean and easy layout
- Gives option to record as activity of all scored sport types, but is optional

## Scorekeeping Options

- Keeps track of score stored in device memory, so even if app leaves score is not reset
- Score never resets unless menu is opened and option 'reset' is selected
- Option to change names for Home and Guest

## Activity Recording

- If activity is started, show timer and score in slightly different layout
- Supports lap option
- Tracks score changes and records as special data in Connect IQ

### Sport Profiles

*Special types of scoring systems will be accounted for and programmed to work for that sport type, including determining whose serve it is, or who has ball, etc., but this is optional. Everything will default to simplest scoring options, and primary scoring system will be a generic activity profile, but by default, no activity recording is set*

- Generic VS (Home vs. Guest style game)
- Generic Group (Can record up to 10 people for scoring different types)
- Custom Activity (set name in settings)
